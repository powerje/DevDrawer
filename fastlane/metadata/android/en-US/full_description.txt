DevDrawer is the ultimate home screen widget designed specifically for Android developers.

Tired of cluttering your home screen with multiple build variants of your app? DevDrawer helps you organize and access your installed applications using powerful regular expression (regex) patterns.

**Key Features:**
*   **Pattern-Based Filtering:** Simply define a regex pattern (e.g., `com.example.*`) and DevDrawer will automatically populate with all matching installed apps.
*   **Automatic Updates:** The widget listens for package installation and removal events, ensuring your list is always up-to-date without manual refreshing.
*   **Quick Actions:** Long-press or tap to access essential developer shortcuts:
    *   App Info (Clear Data/Cache)
    *   Uninstall
    *   Open in Play Store
    *   Copy Package Name
*   **Customizable:** Create multiple widgets with different patterns for different projects.

Streamline your development workflow and keep your device organized with DevDrawer.

Permissions:
*   `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`: Required to detect and list the applications installed on your device that match your rules.

Open Source and built with modern Android standards (Compose, Glance).
