# Privacy Policy for DevDrawer

**Last Updated:** December 5, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how DevDrawer ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our application.

## Information Collection and Use

**DevDrawer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal user data.**

The application operates entirely locally on your device. It does not have an internet connection permission (unless implicitly granted by libraries, but we do not use it for data collection).

### Installed Application Inventory
DevDrawer requests the `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES` permission to function. This permission allows the app to:
1.  **List installed applications:** To compare them against the Regular Expression patterns you provide.
2.  **Display app details:** To show the app icon and name in the widget.
3.  **Launch apps:** To allow you to open the apps directly from the widget.

**This data is processed locally on your device and is never shared with us or any third parties.** The list of your installed applications never leaves your phone.

## Third-Party Services
DevDrawer does not use any third-party analytics or advertising services.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
